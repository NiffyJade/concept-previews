Questions

Good to know.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes — new patients are welcome. Use the booking form above or call (512) 615-9405.

Do you take my insurance?

We work with many insurance plans. Call the office with your plan details and we'll confirm your coverage before your visit.

What is SMART mercury removal?

SMART is a protocol for safely removing mercury amalgam fillings. Ask Dr. Su or Dr. Jett during a consultation whether it's right for you.

What if I need to be seen urgently?

Choose “Toothache / urgent” in the booking form, or call us during office hours.
